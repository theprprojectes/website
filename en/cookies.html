Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This website uses cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience. Below we explain what cookies are, what types we use and how you can manage them.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and improve how it works.

2. Types of Cookies We Use

Type Purpose Duration
Technical / Essential Enable basic website functionality (navigation, forms). Session
Analytics Collect anonymous statistics about how visitors use the site (e.g. Google Analytics). Up to 2 years
Third-party Embedded content such as YouTube videos may set their own cookies. Varies

3. Managing Cookies

You can manage or disable cookies through your browser settings. Here are the links for the most common browsers:

  • Google Chrome
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Safari
  • Microsoft Edge

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain parts of the website.

4. Updates to This Policy
