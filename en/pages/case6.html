JULIA CRY

Comprehensive PR plan (2024–2026): "Noche de Damas", RTVE Play "Feat" and MIN Awards

Objective: Build a structured communications strategy to consolidate the project, organise the narrative, develop the artistic brand and establish a solid relationship with media.

Key milestones covered:

  • Release of single "Noche de Damas" (2024).
  • Participation in music talent show "Feat" (RTVE Play) (2025).
  • Nominations at the Premios Yepes de la Música de la Región de Murcia (2025) and Premios MIN (2026).

Client: Julia Cry

Service: Public Relations

Markets: ES

Years: 2024–2026

Emerging urban artist following her debut album "INSISTO" (2024). We developed a comprehensive plan to organise the narrative, build the artistic brand and turn milestones into communications assets, expanding reach to music, cultural and general media.

CAMPAIGN 1 — "NOCHE DE DAMAS" (2024)

Objective: The project's first PR campaign. Beyond working the single launch, we introduced Julia Cry to the media, contextualising her artistic proposition and sonic identity within the national urban scene.

What we did

Launch and media introduction of the project

We designed the communications and PR strategy for the launch, developed the single's narrative within the artistic universe, wrote and distributed the press release and managed relationships with specialist music, cultural and general media.

Results

Coverage in digital and general media

We achieved presence in key digital music outlets (What Magazine, Urban Roosters) and regional press with local and national audiences, laying the foundations for future campaigns.

Selected hits:

What Magazine
Urban Roosters — profile / career
  • La Verdad de Murcia — interview
  • eldiario.es — Murcia

CAMPAIGN 2 — RTVE PLAY "FEAT" + AWARD NOMINATIONS

We worked Julia Cry's participation in the music programme "Feat" as a visibility and legitimacy milestone, extending reach to broader general audiences. In parallel, we turned nominations and awards such as the Premios Yepes de la Música de la Región de Murcia and Premios MIN into a communications asset to reinforce credibility with media, industry and public alike.

What we did

Visibility, legitimation and media narrative

  • Communications strategy around her participation in the talent show "Feat" + nominations at music awards.
  • Writing and distribution of press releases to regional and national media.
  • Coordination of media hits and placements.
  • Reinforcement of positioning as an emerging artist with projection.

Milestones:

  • Winner of two awards — "Best Emerging Artist" and "Best Urban Music Album" (Insisto) — at the Premios Yepes de la Música de la Región de Murcia (2025).
  • In 2026, Julia Cry received six nominations at the Premios MIN, reinforcing the continuity and strength of the artistic project.

RESULTS

  • Increased media visibility in digital music and cultural outlets (Urban Roosters, What Magazine, Binary, La Estrofa, among others).
  • News coverage of awards and nominations reinforcing credibility (La Verdad, La Opinión de Murcia, Murcia Plaza, among others).
  • Construction of a clear artistic brand narrative and foundations for future campaigns.
  • Strengthened media relationships, facilitating future communications actions.
  • Positioning of Julia Cry as a relevant emerging artist within the current music landscape.
