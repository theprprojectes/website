ALEJANDRA AZCÁRATE

PR Campaign "What Is Permitted, Is Repeated" (2025)

Objective: Promote Alejandra Azcárate's European tour in Spain — with dates in Tenerife, Madrid, Málaga, Barcelona and Bilbao — boosting media visibility for the show Lo Que Se Permite, Se Repite and reinforcing her positioning in the Spanish cultural and theatre market.

Approach: Campaign tailored to the tour route, combining national and local media hits according to each tour city.

Narrative: Focus on the show's message, its social dimension and its connection with audiences, contextualising the tour as a culturally significant event on Spain's theatre and comedy calendar.

Client: Alejandra Azcárate

Service: Public Relations

Markets: ES

Year: 2025

We designed a PR strategy tailored to the tour route, combining national and local media hits per city. We delivered a campaign based on newsworthy content and interviews in general and specialist media, in both traditional and digital formats. Coverage was progressive and sustained, accompanying each milestone of the tour.

LO QUE SE PERMITE, SE REPITE (2025)

Objective: Boost the show's media visibility in the Spanish press and position Alejandra Azcárate as one of the most recognised voices in Latin stand-up, reinforcing her presence in Spain's cultural and theatre market.

Cities: Tenerife, Madrid, Málaga, Barcelona and Bilbao.

What we did

City-by-city strategy and newsworthy narrative

Campaign tailored to the tour route, combining national and local media hits. Development of newsworthy content and interviews with presence in general and specialist media, in both traditional and digital formats. The narrative focused on the show's message, its social dimension and its connection with audiences.

Media plan

Progressive and sustained coverage

The plan delivered progressive and sustained coverage, accompanying each milestone of the tour and consolidating the project's visibility throughout the campaign, positioning the show as a reference point for Latin stand-up in Spain in 2025.

Results: 60+ hits across national, local and specialist media.

  • Back page in Grupo Joly (print).
  • Interview with EFE Sevilla, with coverage in national media including ABC and Infobae, as well as international outlets such as La Patria, El Siglo and Swiss Info, among others.
  • Appearances in listings, general and digital media with wide reach, as well as specialist press covering culture and entertainment.
  • Publication of interviews and news pieces that contributed to positioning the tour as one of the benchmarks for Latin stand-up in Spain in 2025.

Featured hits:

EFE Sevilla
ABC Cultura
Diario de Sevilla (Grupo Joly)
La Cresta de la Onda (RTVE)

RESULTS

  • 60+ hits across national, local and specialist media.
  • City-by-city coverage (Tenerife, Madrid, Málaga, Barcelona, Bilbao) with sustained visibility throughout the campaign.
  • Reinforced positioning in cultural and entertainment press, in both traditional and digital formats.
