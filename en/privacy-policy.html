Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

In compliance with Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and Organic Law 3/2018 on the Protection of Personal Data (LOPDGDD), we inform you of the following regarding the processing of your personal data.

1. Data Controller

The PR Project
Email: hola@theprproject.es
Location: Madrid, Spain

2. Data We Collect

Through the contact form on our website, we collect the following personal data:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Message content

3. Purpose of Processing

The data you provide is used exclusively to:

  • Respond to enquiries or requests sent via the contact form.
  • Manage the commercial relationship if you become a client.

4. Legal Basis

The legal basis for processing is your explicit consent, provided when you submit the contact form. You may withdraw this consent at any time.

5. Data Retention

Data will be retained for the time necessary to respond to your enquiry and, where applicable, for the duration of the commercial relationship, plus the legally required retention periods.

6. Data Sharing

Your data will not be transferred to third parties, except where legally required or where service providers acting as data processors are involved (e.g. email hosting), always under the appropriate data processing agreements.

7. Your Rights

You may exercise the following rights by writing to hola@theprproject.es:

  • Access: know what data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: correct inaccurate data.
  • Erasure: request deletion of your data.
  • Restriction: limit the processing of your data.
  • Portability: receive your data in a structured format.
  • Objection: object to the processing of your data.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) at www.aepd.es.

8. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures to protect your personal data against unauthorised access, loss or alteration.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be published on this page with the updated date.
